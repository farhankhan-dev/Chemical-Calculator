PRIVACY POLICY FOR CHEMI CALC

Effective Date: 2026-08-20

This Privacy Policy describes how Chemi Calc ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. 

1. ZERO DATA COLLECTION
Chemi Calc is designed with privacy as its absolute core principle. We do not collect, transmit, store externally, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

2. LOCAL STORAGE ONLY
All data you generate or enter within the App (such as custom chemicals, formulas, and notes in the "My Chemicals" notebook) is stored entirely locally on your device using local storage mechanisms. 
- Your data never leaves your device.
- We have no access to your data.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated into this App.

3. PLAY STORE DATA SAFETY
In compliance with the Google Play Store Data Safety guidelines, we declare that:
- No data is collected
- No data is shared with third parties
- Data is not encrypted in transit because data is never transmitted.
- You have full control over your data. To delete all your data, you can simply clear the app's cache/data through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Because we do not collect any personal information, the App is completely safe for use by children of all ages. 

5. PERMISSIONS
The App does not request any intrusive device permissions (like Camera, Microphone, or Location). Any permissions requested are strictly for essential local file operations (e.g., exporting your chemical notebook to a text file on your device).

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the functionality of the App changes. Any updates will be reflected in this document. Since we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you of changes; we encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. CONTACT US
If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the support email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
