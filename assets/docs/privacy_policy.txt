Privacy Policy for ChemiCalc

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction
Welcome to ChemiCalc. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. 

2. Information Collection and Use
ChemiCalc is designed to be an offline-first, privacy-focused application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or usage data with our servers. All calculations, searches, and data processing occur locally on your device.

3. Local Storage and Custom Data
The App allows you to add custom chemical formulas and save them to your "My Chemicals" list.
- All custom chemical data is stored entirely locally on your device.
- We do not have access to your custom chemicals, nor are they synced to any cloud servers by us.
- If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your locally stored custom chemicals will be deleted.

4. File Access and Permissions
To enable the "Import/Export" feature for your custom chemicals, the App interacts with your device's file system.
- Importing: When you import a file, you do so through the secure system file picker. We only access the specific file you explicitly choose to import.
- Exporting: When you export your data, the App writes a text file to your device's public Downloads directory. 
- The App does not require broad storage permissions on modern Android versions (Scoped Storage) and does not scan, read, or modify any other files or folders on your device.

5. Third-Party Services
Since ChemiCalc operates offline and does not collect personal data, it does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising networks that track user behavior.
However, the App is distributed via the Google Play Store, which may collect basic app usage and crash reporting data in accordance with Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Security
The security of your data is important to us. Because your data remains exclusively on your device, it is protected by your device’s built-in security features (such as PIN, password, or biometric locks). We take reasonable measures within the App's code to ensure that custom chemical exports and imports are sanitized to prevent data corruption. However, remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of exposing children's personal information.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

9. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via the support email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
